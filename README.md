# streets-of-simcity-custom-scenarios
Streets of SimCity Custom Scenarios

This is a repository for the scenarios I have made for the 1997 Maxis game Streets of SimCity.  In the early 2000s, I had started reverse engineering the scenario format for this game, and got maybe halfway there, and moved on to other things such as college.  Once COVID hit, @CahootsMalone separately deciphered the scenario format, which was immensely useful to help me complete the ideas I had had decades prior.  Since then, I have made a few more scenarios as well, and want to share them.  Shoutout to Alek Krimsky as well, for hosting my early scenarios on his website!  http://www.streetsofsimcity.com/maps.html

The Releases tab should contain a .zip file with all the scenarios and associated cities, to be updated as more may get made in the future.

Some notes about Streets of SimCity Scenarios:

Traffic

Generally speaking, AIs who are not Hunters, Racers, Bosses, (or Cops when chasing you) will wander along the city streets in the right lane of each road tile, regardless of whether it is a road or part of a highway.  This behavior is always the case, unless an AI drives into a tunnel.  When this occurs, they automatically move over the centerline of the road.  This is kindof odd behavior, but I suspect it is a quick "bugfix" to deal with the horribly cursed collision at tunnel entrances and walls, so that AIs are less prone to get stuck at these areas as often or clip through the walls, leading to falling into an Unrecoverable Error has Occurred.  However, when AIs *exit* a tunnel, nobody programmed the AIs go back to the right side of the road, meaning they are stuck driving straight down the middle of the road until they are killed or despawn.  In a city setting, this results in AIs basically driving into every other AI, and just generally looking unpolished.  Because of this, if you want to have "pedestrian" traffic that flows more realistically, your city either needs to not have any tunnels (such as in my Bomb Squad scenario), or you need to set the range of each traffic AI limited to prevent them from ever reaching a tunnel (such as in my SimFlags scenario).

Cops

Cops seem to have mundane traffic behavior like the other benign AIs (Courier, Speeder, Ally), until either you attack one, bump into one, or drive over the speed limit.  However, that speed limit is not actually defined as 55 MPH in the game itself, but is defined within the entry of each Cop AI; i.e. within each AI definition, you set the speed at which that Cop will start chasing you.  This is set by Speed1 in CahootsMalone's scenario format document.  An example of this is the security guards in SimFlags; Speed1 is set to 200 MPH, meaning most cars *cannot* trigger a Cop to chase you, allowing you to roam the park at whatever speed you wish without being hassled - but they will chase you if you attack them.  But, since each AI can have a different "speed limit" set for them, if you also give each Cop a range, you effectively set a speed limit for a particular region of the map that they can occupy.  An actual example of this that I hadn't realized was implemented in game is in "01 Qualifier" in Race for your Life; as a kid, I assumed the informational messages about Speed limits was just for grins due to it always being 55 MPH in Player's Choice, and any time you break a posted speed limit, you're probably doing at least 160 MPH and if you evade the first Cop, all the other Cops will be looking for you regardless of your speed, so I assumed they all had the same behavior as the first.  But, in the scenario file, they do in fact have different Speed1 values and radii, meaning if you were to follow the posted speed limits, they wouldn't follow you (or if you got a ticket and ended the chase, the next encountered Cop would use its own speed limit to decide to chase you).

Races

These are janky.  A scenario is defined as a race iff the LAPS value is non-zero.  If it's zero, Racer AIs will be broken, checkpoints won't spawn (though you can still drive on them and receive a "Wrong Checkpoint" message), and mission-win and mission-lose event groups behave normally.  But if it is non-zero, a few things happen:
- Racer AIs will work correctly, and spawn at the starting line
- You will spawn at the starting line, no matter what LOCX and LOCY say
- Mission-win event groups can exist and trigger, but they can no longer win the scenario.  That said, labels and event order hierarchy still applies like in non-race scenarios, including the location flag on the map
- Mission-lose event groups can exist and trigger, but act like informational groups
- You only lose if you run out of time.  If TIME is set to 0, you can't lose
- You win iff you complete the number of laps in LAPS
- If LAPS is non-zero but no CHCK definitions exist, the game crashes with an Unrecoverable Error

The only way to win is to complete the number of laps defined in the scenario; there is no way to force a win event as mission-win groups do not win anymore and you can't arbitrarily alter the number of laps completed during game.  You can however force a losing condition: although mission-lose groups have no effect, any individual event on its own can have a Money bonus and a Time bonus.  These are both signed integers, so you could define an event that when satisfied gives you a negative time bonus about as large as the TIME limit defined at the beginning of the scenario.  This will reduce your remaining time to a negative number, which if the negative number isn't too large, will be interpreted as running out of time, triggering the Lose condition.  This is demonstrated in the SimFlags scenario: if you destroy a security guard or deliver the Easter egg, you lose (with some extra dramatic text to appear before the "Time Is Up" text can).

As a sidenote, it seems that if a negative time bonus is sufficiently large, something probably overflows in the game logic and it stops timing you- effectively removing the time limit mid-scenario.  An example of this is in the Rally Cross scenario, after you complete enough jumps in the race and exit to the Winners' Circle, you can go to the Mini Mall where there is a package.  Driving to that location of that package triggers an event that deducts so much time from the clock that the clock stops and the Time Left notification in the Scenario Progress window disappears, allowing you to freely roam about the adjacent city without a time limit.

Racers

A couple notes about Racer AIs: if you are in a non-race scenario, they spawn at -1, -1 and crawl along the Y axis until they reach the end of the map, doing nothing useful, no matter what you define in their spawn location.
In an actual race, they also ignore the spawn location and instead always spawn at the starting line, including Racers who spawn after an event group completes or after a certain amount of time.  For Racers, Speed1 appears to be the speed at which they move along the track if you are idle or not moving, and Speed2 appears to be the speed they will go if you are driving flat-out.  It seems they may adjust between these two speeds depending on your speed between 0 MPH and flat-out.

Explosion effects

Starting with the Urban Loop race, many of my scenarios have taken advantage of jumping over a building as it explodes.  This is achieved through defining a Reach Location event that triggers as you are entering the jump over a building.  This one event is tied to its own Event Group that then completes.  In the AI definitions, there is a Rogue that will spawn next to said building when this Event Group completes, whose target is where it spawned.  This leads to the Rogue immediately exploding, blowing up the building before the Rogue can even be rendered.  As a result, you essentially start to jump over the building as it starts burning down making a dramatic jump over flames (at which point the building no longer has collision - so while in Urban Loop you jump over a flaming Cargo Bay, you could jump through a larger building if it is already exploding as you enter its tile).  You may notice when doing these jumps/stunts in these scenarios that you can occasionally hear a Machine Gun blast or see bullet damage on your car- this was from the Rogue that spawned to create the effect.
